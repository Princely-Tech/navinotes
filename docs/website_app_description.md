# NaviNotes – Study Companion & Smart Note-Taking App

## 1. One‑line Pitch (Tagline)
NaviNotes is a modern study companion that helps students turn messy course materials into organized boards, visual mind maps, and smart flashcards—backed by spaced repetition and powerful revision tools.

## 2. Short Marketing Blurb (2–3 sentences)
NaviNotes brings all your study materials into one place so you can stay on top of classes, exams, and projects. Create beautiful study boards, capture notes, attach files, and turn concepts into flashcards and mind maps in seconds. With a built‑in Pomodoro timer, recent activity feed, and cross‑device sync via the backend, NaviNotes is your all‑in‑one hub for productive learning.

## 3. Who NaviNotes Is For (Target Users)
- **Students** preparing for exams, quizzes, and standardized tests.
- **University & college learners** managing multiple courses, readings, and assignments.
- **Researchers & knowledge workers** organizing references, PDFs, and complex ideas.
- **Self‑learners** studying online courses, certifications, and personal projects.

## 4. Core Concept: Boards & Content
NaviNotes is built around **boards** and **content**.

- **Boards**
  - Create separate boards for each **course, subject, project, or exam**.
  - Choose from curated visual themes: **Plain, Minimalist, Dark Academia, Light Academia, Nature**.
  - Add course information (subject, level, term, timelines, syllabus links) so each board becomes a mini command‑center for that topic.
  - Attach a custom cover image so every board is visually distinct and easy to recognize.

- **Content Types on a Board**
  - **Notes** – rich text notes for lectures, summaries, worked examples, and revisions.
  - **Mind map nodes** – visual nodes that live on a board’s mind‑map canvas and connect related ideas.
  - **Flashcard decks** – question–answer cards tied to specific topics or boards, used for active recall.
  - **Files & media** – attach PDFs, documents, images and more (e.g. `pdf, docx, ppt, xls, jpg, png`).
  - **Voice notes (per content)** – attach audio snippets to notes or documents for quick verbal explanations.

Each piece of content is time‑stamped, tagged, and linked to its board, making it easy to filter by topic, last‑edited time, and content type.

## 5. Key Features Overview
- **Dashboard** – see your boards, recent activity, and quick access to what matters now.
- **Recent Notes & Recent Activity** – instantly jump back into your most recently edited notes, files, mind‑maps, and flashcards across all boards.
- **Board Themes & Layouts** – choose from multiple aesthetic templates tailored for different learning styles.
- **Mind Map Study Mode** – visually connect concepts using a mind‑map canvas per board.
- **Flashcards with Spaced Repetition** – create and study flashcard decks powered by spaced‑repetition logic.
- **PDF & File Support** – upload and view course PDFs and study files directly in the app.
- **Pomodoro Timer** – run focused study sessions with built‑in Pomodoro support.
- **Study Marketplace** – buy and sell curated study boards and content.
- **User Profile & Onboarding** – personalize the app around your study goals and background.
- **Cross‑device sync (via backend API)** – boards and content sync with the backend for backup and multi‑device use.

## 6. Detailed Feature Descriptions

### 6.1 Smart Dashboard
- **Personalized home screen** that loads your boards and latest work as soon as you sign in.
- **Recent activity feed** showing your most recently updated notes, mind‑map nodes, flashcard decks, and files.
- **Quick actions** to create new boards, open existing ones, or jump into flashcards.
- Clean, responsive layout that adapts from mobile up to desktop screens.

### 6.2 Boards for Each Course or Project
- **Create a board per subject, course, exam, or project** so nothing gets mixed up.
- Add optional **course info** such as subject, level, term, and syllabus.
- Use **board timelines** to model the flow of a course or project over time.
- Customize the **look and feel** of your boards with preset themes:
  - Plain – distraction‑free white workspace.
  - Minimalist – clean lines and essential elements only.
  - Dark Academia – warm, moody palette inspired by vintage libraries.
  - Light Academia – bright, scholarly aesthetic with soft, cream tones.
  - Nature – organic shapes and natural colors for calm focus.

### 6.3 Rich Note‑Taking
- Create **structured notes** inside each board for lectures, summaries, formulas, and reviews.
- Attach **tags**, cover images, and voice notes to important content.
- Supported file types for attachments include PDFs, Office documents, plain‑text files, and images.
- Notes are fully searchable and organized by board and last updated time.

### 6.4 Visual Mind Maps
- Every board can be explored as a **mind map**.
- Add **mind‑map nodes** for key concepts, chapters, subtopics, or ideas.
- **Drag nodes around** the canvas to visually arrange relationships.
- Connect nodes with **links** to show how ideas fit together.
- Customize **node colors, shapes, and sizes** for visual hierarchy and memory cues.
- Mind‑map information is stored in content records for better sync and performance.

### 6.5 Flashcards & Spaced Repetition
- Turn your notes and concepts into **flashcard decks** attached to a board.
- Create decks **manually** or use advanced tools (including AI‑assisted flows) to speed up creation.
- Study decks with a dedicated **Flashcard Study** mode built around spaced‑repetition principles.
- Track performance and card difficulty to focus on what you are most likely to forget.
- Organize decks per course so you can rapidly revise before tests and exams.

### 6.6 File & PDF Management
- Upload **PDFs and other study files** into boards.
- View PDFs directly in the app with a dedicated PDF viewer.
- Attach lecture slides, assignments, rubrics, and readings to the relevant board.
- Keep all documents for a course in one place instead of scattered across folders.

### 6.7 Study Marketplace (Optional Advanced Feature)
- Access a **marketplace** of community‑created boards and study resources.
- Browse, preview, and purchase **ready‑made study boards** (notes, mind maps, flashcards, and files) for specific subjects and exams.
- Turn your best boards into **products** in your own store.
- Track **purchases and uploads** through dedicated “My Store” and “My Purchases” sections.

### 6.8 Productivity Tools: Pomodoro & Notifications
- Built‑in **Pomodoro timer** screen to run focused study sessions with breaks.
- **Notifications center** for reminders, study streaks, and system messages.

### 6.9 Onboarding & Personalization
- On first use, NaviNotes asks about:
  - Your name and role (e.g. high‑school student, undergrad, researcher, self‑learner).
  - School name, field of study, and education level.
  - Why you are using the app: exams, projects, research, brainstorming, or course notes.
- This information is used to **personalize the experience** and future recommendations.
- Update your **profile, photo, and study preferences** anytime from the profile screen.

### 6.10 Recent Notes & Activity Across Boards
- Dedicated **Recent Notes** screen that pulls your latest work from all boards.
- Shows a **unified list** of most recently updated notes, mind‑map nodes, flashcard decks, and files.
- Each item displays an icon, content type, last‑updated timestamp, and quick navigation.
- Perfect for getting back into where you left off, even if you forgot which board it was on.

## 7. Cross‑Platform Experience
- Built with **Flutter** for a seamless experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Responsive layout components ensure the app feels at home on small screens and large monitors.
- Data synced via backend APIs and local storage, so you can **keep working offline** and sync when reconnected.

## 8. Why NaviNotes Is Different
- **All‑in‑one study hub** – combines boards, notes, mind maps, flashcards, and files instead of making you juggle multiple apps.
- **Designed around real courses** – boards reflect how students actually think about classes: by subject, topic, and syllabus.
- **Visual + textual learning** – supports both linear note‑taking and spatial mind‑mapping.
- **Built‑in active recall** – flashcards and spaced repetition are first‑class citizens, not an afterthought.
- **Aesthetic, theme‑based workspaces** – Dark Academia, Light Academia, Minimalist, Nature, and Plain for different moods and personalities.
